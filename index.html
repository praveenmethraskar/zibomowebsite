Who We Are

Technology-Driven Storage Automation

Zibomo is a smart locker technology and manufacturing company building secure, automated storage solutions for businesses and public spaces.

We combine industrial locker hardware, electronic locking systems and cloud-connected software to replace manual keys, tokens and registers with controlled digital workflows.

Our modular systems are configured around available space, expected footfall and operational requirements.
