Zibomo Use Cases

Zibomo Smart Lockers is the best solution to transform time-consuming manual tasks into automated processes. Built with advanced digital technology beyond the simple keyless entry, Zibomo Smart Lockers offer fully integrated reporting and real-time management.

Logistics

Zibomo Smart Lockers can automate the management of high-value devices, reducing loss, damage, and management time. Personal storage lockers can likewise assist you in handling allocated space for enormous and fast-moving workforces — on a shift-by-shift basis.

Residential

Zibomo Smart Lockers offer residents a safe and secure, convenient, and trustworthy mail retrieval option that puts experience first. They can even be configured to adjust large items and deliveries, furnishing a suitable and practical solution for residents.

E-Commerce

E-Commerce smart lockers permit customers to benefit from the ease of shopping online and offer instantaneous, flexible collections. An order is allocated to a locker, and the customer will be automatically informed when it's ready for pick up. Offering a user-friendly and unique click-and-collect experience, Zibomo Smart Lockers can restore and refresh your shop floor.

Laundry

People want convenience, and this business model offers the ultimate laundry service with minimum effort. This can create an easy residual income for a hotel or property manager with little to no effort. It likewise provides an efficient and time-saving service to your guests or residents.

Schools / Universities

Zibomo university lockers can create a safe and secure space for their belongings in class, the gym, the library, or anywhere else on campus. With more students carrying precious cargo like expensive phones and computers, sports equipment, and class projects or portfolios, having an effortless, connected way for students and faculty to ensure their items are safe throughout the day.
