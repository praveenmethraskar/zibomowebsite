Smart Lockers: Common Questions

What buyers and venue operators ask us most often about digital smart lockers.

A digital smart locker is a bank of steel compartments controlled by an electronic locking system and a central touchscreen terminal instead of physical keys. The user selects a compartment size, verifies their mobile number by OTP, creates a four-digit PIN and completes payment where it applies, and the allocated locker opens automatically. To collect, they enter the same mobile number and PIN. Every transaction is recorded on a cloud platform, so operators see availability, usage and locker status without staffing a counter.

Cost depends on the number of compartments, the compartment mix, the enclosure specification for indoor or outdoor use, and the software plan. Zibomo supplies lockers on outright purchase as well as on a managed model where the hardware and locker management platform are provided together. Share your location, available floor space and expected daily users and we will send a written configuration and commercial proposal.

Yes. High-footfall public venues are one of the main use cases. Visitors store bags, footwear, mobile phones or shopping in a self-service locker, pay at the terminal, and collect using their mobile number and PIN. This replaces manual cloakroom counters, token registers and the staffing they need, and it gives the venue a record of every deposit and collection.

The courier or security desk deposits the parcel into a compartment against the resident's mobile number. The resident receives a PIN or OTP, walks up to the locker at any hour, enters their mobile number and code, and the compartment opens. Security staff stop holding parcels, residents stop chasing deliveries, and every handover is timestamped.

Compartments are available in Small, Medium, Large, XL and custom sizes, and locker banks are configured around the space available, the expected user volume and the items being stored. The terminal architecture is expandable, so additional locker banks can be added to an existing installation as demand grows.
